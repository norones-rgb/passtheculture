Pass The Culture

Support

Pick a deck, split into teams, pass the phone, and see who really knows the culture. Pass The Culture works offline on one phone, with no accounts.

Contact

Email isginllc@gmail.com. We read every message. You can also use Settings → Send feedback in the app, which adds your app version so we can help faster.

Common questions

I bought a deck, but it isn't showing up.

Open Settings → Restore purchases while connected to the internet, using the same Google account (or Apple ID) you bought with. Once restored, your decks work offline.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, so refunds are handled by them: see Google Play refunds or Apple refunds.

A card is wrong, outdated, or offensive.

After a turn, tap Review cards, then Report on that card. You can also Hide it so it never appears again. Every card is reviewed by people, and reports go straight to the team that maintains them.

Does it need the internet?

No. Games work fully offline. You only need a connection to buy or restore decks.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset all local data, or uninstall the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
