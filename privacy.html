Pass The Culture

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Pass The Culture is a party game that runs on one phone. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: your games stay on your phone, there are no accounts and no ads, and we never sell your information.

Pass The Culture is published by Ibrahima Aw ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: isginllc@gmail.com.

Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device. We never receive it.

  • Your settings, such as sound, vibration, text size, and the family-friendly filter.
  • Games in progress and summaries of finished games, including the team names you type.
  • Which cards you have already seen, so the game can show you fresh ones, and any cards you choose to hide.
  • A record of which decks you own, so purchased decks work without an internet connection.

You can delete all of this at any time with Settings → Reset all local data, or by uninstalling the app. Your phone's own backup service (for example, Android backup) may include this data in your device backups; that backup is controlled by your device settings and your Google account, not by us.

Purchases

Premium decks are one-time purchases. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone). We never see your card number or other payment details.

To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a service provider that processes purchase records on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier created by the app (not your name, email, or any advertising identifier),
  • your purchase and transaction records from the app store,
  • basic technical information such as the app version, device type, operating system version, and country or region, and
  • your IP address, as part of any internet connection.

This information is used only to process purchases, unlock the decks you bought, restore them on a new device, and handle refunds. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Feedback and card reports

If you choose to send feedback or report a card, the app opens your own email app with a message you can read and edit before sending. The message includes the app version, the content version, your phone's platform, and, for reports, the card's ID and the reason you picked. It never includes team names. When you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them only to reply and to fix the problem.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access.
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting at this time. If we add either, it will be anonymous, it will never include what you type or team names, it will have an off switch in Settings, and we will update this policy first.
  • We never sell or rent your information.

Children

Pass The Culture is a party game for teens and adults, rated for ages 12 and up. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting information

Information on your phone stays until you reset the app or uninstall it. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and the app stores are kept as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal and accounting obligations. To ask us to delete purchase records linked to your anonymous identifier, email isginllc@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as access or deletion; contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
